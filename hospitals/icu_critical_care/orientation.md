# Intensive Care Unit (ICU) — Critical Care Clinical Orientation Guide

## 1. Central Venous Line (CVC) & Arterial Line Standards
- **Central Line Placement**: Full sterile drape, chlorhexidine prep 2-minute dry time, Biopatch applied blue-to-sky.
- **Arterial Line**: Level transducer strictly to the **phlebostatic axis** (4th intercostal space, mid-axillary line). Perform fast-flush square-wave test each shift.

---

## 2. Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy (CRRT)
- **Prismaflex Setup**: Prime set with 2 Liters saline. Verify replacement fluid bags and effluent scale balance.
- **Anticoagulation**: Regional citrate anticoagulation protocol requires post-filter ionized calcium checks q2h.
- **Fluid Warmer**: Blood warmer cuff must be positioned on the return line to prevent hypothermia.

---

## 3. Mechanical Ventilation & Extubation Protocols
- **Vent Weaning SBT**: Daily Spontaneous Breathing Trial (SBT) at 0600 on PS 5 / PEEP 5 if sedation turned off.
- **Subglottic Suctioning**: Connect ETT Hi-Lo evacuation port to continuous low wall suction (-20 to -30 mmHg).
