# ICU Staff Nurse — Critical Care Management Guide
**Facility:** Duke Regional Hospital | **Unit:** Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

## 1. Hemodynamic Monitoring & Vasoactive Drip Titration
- Maintain MAP >= 65 mmHg (or individualized target for neuro-ICU patients).
- First-line in septic shock: Norepinephrine (Levophed). Add Vasopressin 0.03 units/min for refractory hypotension.
- Arterial line leveling to phlebostatic axis (4th intercostal space, mid-axillary line) every shift and after repositioning.

## 2. Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy (CRRT)
- Prismaflex setup: check regional citrate anticoagulation (RCA) protocol.
- Monitor post-filter ionized calcium (target 0.25 - 0.35 mmol/L) and systemic iCa (target 1.1 - 1.3 mmol/L).

## 3. Ventilator Bundle & Sedation Interruption
- Daily Spontaneous Awakening Trial (SAT) and Spontaneous Breathing Trial (SBT).
- Subglottic suctioning q4h; oral care with 0.12% Chlorhexidine twice daily.
