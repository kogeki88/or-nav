# Duke Regional Hospital — Clinical Practice Guidelines & Facility Protocols

## 1. Safety Alerts & Emergency Actions
- **Emergency Dispatch (All Codes)**: Call **x6-9111**. Specify Building, Floor, Room number.
- **Code Blue (Adult)**: Overhead activation + rapid response pager.
- **Code Stork (OB/Neonatal Emergency)**: Floor 3 Labor & Delivery STAT team.
- **Stroke Alert**: CT Scanner 2 cleared immediately. Neurology consult via Stroke-Net pager **x6-3300**.

## 2. Essential Clinical Phone Extensions
- **Duke Regional Blood Bank STAT**: **x6-2244**.
- **Central Pharmacy / Pyxis Support**: **x6-1800**.
- **Surgical Suite Front Desk (Control Desk)**: **x6-5400**.
- **Infection Prevention & Control**: **x6-4422**.
- **Hospital House Supervisor**: **x6-0000**.

## 3. Duke Health Safety Standards
- Barcode Medication Administration (BCMA) threshold >= 98% compliance required.
- Two patient identifiers verified on every phlebotomy specimen at the draw bedside.
- Strict surgical attire laundering: hospital issued blue scrubs, clean lab coats removed before entering restricted semi-sterile corridors.
