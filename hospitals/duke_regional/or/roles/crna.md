# CRNA Clinical Guide — Duke Regional Hospital
**Department:** Anesthesiology | **High-Acuity Case Management**

## 1. Steep Trendelenburg & Pneumoperitoneum Physiology
- Hypercarbia & Acidosis: Increase minute ventilation by 20-30% to compensate for diaphragmatic splinting and CO2 absorption.
- Facial & Airway Edema: Perform cuff-leak test prior to extubation after prolonged (>3 hr) robotic steep Trendelenburg cases.

## 2. Neuro & Spine Monitoring Precautions
- Motor Evoked Potentials (MEP) & Somatosensory Evoked Potentials (SSEP): Total Intravenous Anesthesia (TIVA) using Propofol and Remifentanil. Zero neuromuscular blockade (paralytics) after induction.
