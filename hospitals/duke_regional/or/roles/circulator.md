# Circulating RN Guide — Duke Regional Hospital OR
**Department:** Operating Room | **Safety, Monitoring & Positioning**

## 1. Prone & Steep Trendelenburg Positioning Safeties
- **Steep Trendelenburg**: Apply anti-skid memory foam pad (Pink Pad). Arms tucked at sides with foam sleds. Re-check eye pressure and face clearance periodically.
- **Wilson Frame (Prone)**: Ensure abdomen is completely free from frame crossbars to minimize inferior vena cava compression and bleeding.

## 2. Robotic Docking & Console Coordination
- Maintain minimum 18-inch clearance between robot arms and anesthesia ventilator circuit.
- Emergency Undocking Drill: Press Emergency Stop on patient cart; release instrument clutch buttons before moving boom.
