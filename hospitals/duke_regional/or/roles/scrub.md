# Scrub Tech Guide — Duke Regional Hospital OR
**Department:** Operating Room | **Specialties:** Robotics, Orthopedics, Neuro & Spine

## 1. Robotic Surgery (da Vinci Xi) Draping & Arm Balancing
- Check sterile drape seals on all 4 arms. Align boom laser targeting over operative quadrant before sterile docking.
- Instrument tips must be inserted under direct laparoscopic visualization at all times.
- Keep camera lens preheated in sterile thermos bottle (50°C sterile water) to prevent fogging.

## 2. Spine & Micro-Instrument Protocols
- Kerrison Rongeurs: Clean footplate bone debris with wet gauze sponge immediately after every bite; hand back clean to surgeon.
- Neuro Cottonoids (Patties): String counting mandatory (packs of 10). Never cut strings off cottonoids.
