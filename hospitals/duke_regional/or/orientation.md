# Operating Room (OR) — Multi-Specialty Clinical Orientation Guide

## 1. Station 12 Pneumatic Tube System
The Main Surgical Suite uses **Station 12** for all pneumatic tube transactions.
### CAN Be Tubed
- Lab specimens (labeled, biohazard bag, cap secured)
- Non-controlled medications
- Small supply packs under 5 lbs

### CANNOT Be Tubed
- **Blood products** — Hand-carry STAT by designated runner.
- **Controlled substances** — Dual-witness courier only.
- **Glass containers or heavy instrument sets**.

---

## 2. Massive Transfusion Protocol (MTP) [ROLE: RN, CRNA]
Activation: Extension **x4-BLOOD (x4-25663)**.
State: "MTP activation, OR [Room], attending surgeon [Name]."
Cooler 1 arrives in <10 min: 6 units pRBC, 4 units FFP, 1 unit Platelets (1:1:1 balanced ratio).

---

## 3. Malignant Hyperthermia (MH) Crisis Protocol [ROLE: RN, CRNA]
- **MH Cart Location**: Main OR Hallway between OR 20 and OR 21 (Red labeled cart).
- **STAT Medication**: **Ryanodex (Dantrolene)** 250mg vial reconstitutes with 5 mL sterile water.
- **Dose**: 2.5 mg/kg IV rapid push; repeat 1 mg/kg every 5 minutes until symptoms abate.
- **Hotline**: 1-800-644-9737 (MHAUS 24/7).

---

## 4. Multi-Specialty Surgical Setup & Equipment [ROLE: CIRCULATOR, SCRUB]
- **General & Laparoscopy**: Trocars (5mm/12mm Optical), LigaSure Maryland energy sealer, Endo GIA 60mm staplers located in Cart K.
- **Ortho & Neuro**: Stryker System 8 drill batteries in Cart L; Bone Wax and Gelfoam & Thrombin in Cart B.
- **Cardiovascular & Vascular**: DeBakey forceps and Saphenous vein retractors (Weitlaner) in Cart J.

---

## 5. Nursing Documentation & Specimen Policy [ROLE: CIRCULATOR]
- Specimen chain of custody requires dual-RN signoff before pathology runner handoff.
- Sponge, needle, and instrument counts must be performed and documented in EHR prior to cavity closure.
