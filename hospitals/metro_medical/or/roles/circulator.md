# Circulating RN Guide — Metro Medical Center OR
**Department:** Trauma & Vascular Surgery

## 1. Level 1 Trauma OR Mobilization
- Ensure Belmont rapid infuser is primed with 2 units of uncrossmatched O-negative blood in the warmer chamber.
- Cell Saver (Autotransfusion): Connect dual suction lines to autologous blood recovery system.
- Temperature Management: Set room temperature to 75°F (24°C) to prevent trauma triad of death (hypothermia, acidosis, coagulopathy).

## 2. Radiation Safety in Hybrid OR
- All staff must wear lead aprons (0.5 mm lead equivalence) and thyroid shields.
- Position mobile lead shields between X-ray source and circulating staff during angiography runs.
