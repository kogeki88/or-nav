# CRNA Clinical Guide — Metro Medical Center
**Department:** Anesthesiology | **Trauma & Resuscitation**

## 1. Massive Transfusion Protocol (MTP) Activation
- Ratio: 1:1:1 balanced resuscitation (1 unit pRBC : 1 unit FFP : 1 unit Platelets).
- Cryoprecipitate: Administer 10 units for fibrinogen < 150 mg/dL.
- Prevent Hypocalcemia: Check ionized calcium every 30 minutes; give Calcium Chloride 1g IV for iCa < 1.0 mmol/L.

## 2. Invasive Monitoring in Shock States
- Rapid Radial / Femoral Arterial Line placement with ultrasound guidance.
- Central Venous Access: 9 French Multi-Lumen Access Catheter (MAC) for high-flow resuscitation.
