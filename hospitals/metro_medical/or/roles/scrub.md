# Scrub Tech Guide — Metro Medical Center OR
**Department:** Trauma & Hybrid Vascular Surgery

## 1. Trauma Exploratory Laparotomy Rapid Setup
- Have 2 suction lines hooked up with Yankauer tips before patient enters room.
- Lap Sponge Counting: Rapid pre-count; have packs of 5 lap sponges open and moistened with warm saline.
- Vascular Clamps: Satinsky, DeBakey, and aortic cross-clamp ready on Mayo stand for immediate vascular control.

## 2. Hybrid Vascular & Fluoroscopy Protocols
- Maintain wire and catheter hygiene: Flush all lumens with heparinized saline (5000 units / 500 mL NS).
- Stent Graft Delivery: Check size and contralateral limb orientation with surgeon before unsheathing device.
