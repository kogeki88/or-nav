# Emergency Department RN — Trauma & Resuscitation Guide
**Facility:** Metro Medical Center | **Unit:** Emergency Department (ER)

## 1. Emergency Severity Index (ESI) Triage
- ESI 1 (Immediate resuscitation): Unresponsive, pulseless, severe respiratory distress, acute anaphylaxis.
- ESI 2 (Emergent): High-risk situation, confusion/lethargy, severe pain >= 7/10, vitals in danger zone.

## 2. Massive Transfusion Protocol (MTP) & Rapid Infuser
- Call Blood Bank STAT; designate runner for Cooler 1 (6 pRBC, 4 FFP, 1 Platelets).
- Rapid Infuser Belmont/Level 1 primed with blood-safe filter line.

## 3. Rapid Sequence Intubation (RSI) Assist
- SOAP ME check: Suction, Oxygen, Airway (video laryngoscope), Pharmacy (Etomidate/Ketamine + Rocuronium), Monitoring (ETCO2), Equipment.
