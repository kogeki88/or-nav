# Emergency Department (ER) & Trauma Resuscitation Guide

## 1. Trauma Activation & Code Red Resuscitation
- **Level 1 Trauma Alert**: Page overhead and call Trauma STAT **x4-TRAUMA**.
- **Trauma Bay Setup**: Trauma Bay 1 is reserved for unstable penetrating/blunt trauma; Bay 2 is backup/pediatric.
- **RSI Airway Tackle Box**: Stationed at the head of bed in Bay 1 & Bay 2.

---

## 2. Massive Transfusion Protocol (MTP) in Emergency
- **Rapid Cooler**: Blood bank satellite cooler staged in Alcove B. Code: **4-1-1-#**.
- **Belmont Rapid Infuser**: Located between Trauma Bay 1 and 2. Prime with 1000mL normal saline. Target delivery up to 500 mL/min warmed to 37.5°C.

---

## 3. EZ-IO Intraosseous Access Protocol
- **Anatomic Sites**: Proximal humerus (greater tubercle) or proximal tibia (2cm distal/medial to tibial tuberosity).
- **Needle Selection**: Blue 25mm for adult tibia/humerus; Yellow 45mm for excessive tissue/adipose.
