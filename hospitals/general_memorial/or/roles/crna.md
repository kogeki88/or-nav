# CRNA & Anesthesia — Medication, Airway & Emergency Hemodynamics
**Facility:** General Memorial Hospital | **Department:** Operating Room (OR)

## 1. Airway Setup & Difficult Intubation Equipment
- Standard Tray: Video Laryngoscope (GlideScope Core) with Blade 3 and 4, endotracheal tubes (7.0, 7.5, 8.0), stylets, 10 mL syringe.
- Difficult Airway Cart: Located between OR 15 and 16. Contains Fiberoptic bronchoscope, LMA Supreme (#3, #4, #5), Bougie, Cricothyrotomy kit.

## 2. Emergency Crisis Dosing Algorithms
### Malignant Hyperthermia (MH)
- Stop volatile anesthetics and succinylcholine immediately. High-flow 100% O2 (10 L/min).
- **Ryanodex (Dantrolene)**: 2.5 mg/kg IV push. Each 250 mg vial reconstitutes in 5 mL sterile water. Repeat every 5 min until ETCO2 normalizes.
- Call MHAUS 24/7 hotline: 1-800-644-9737.

### Massive Blood Loss / MTP
- Initiate rapid infuser (Belmont or Level 1) with warm line.
- Calcium Chloride 1g IV for every 4 units of citrated blood products.
- Tranexamic Acid (TXA): 1g IV over 10 min, then 1g infusion over 8 hours.

## 3. Vasoactive Continuous Infusions (Smart Pump Profiles)
- **Norepinephrine (Levophed)**: 4 mg in 250 mL D5W (16 mcg/mL). Titrate 0.02 - 0.3 mcg/kg/min for MAP >= 65 mmHg.
- **Phenylephrine (Neo-Synephrine)**: 20 mg in 250 mL NS (80 mcg/mL). Push dose: 100 mcg (1 mL of 100 mcg/mL).
- **Vasopressin**: 20 units in 100 mL NS (0.2 units/mL). Fixed dose: 0.04 units/min.
