# Scrub Tech / Scrub Nurse — Operating Room Case Setup Guide
**Facility:** General Memorial Hospital | **Department:** Operating Room (OR)

## 1. Mayo Stand & Back Table Setup Rules
- **Sharps Neutral Zone**: Magnetic sharps transfer pad strictly enforced for all scalpel and suture handoffs. No hand-to-hand blade passing.
- **Micro-Instrument Care**: Fine vascular Castroviejo needle holders and DeBakey forceps must never clamp heavy needles or drapes. Inspect jaw alignment before case.
- **Suture Mounting Standards**:
  - 7-0 and 6-0 Prolene: Mount 1/3 from swage on micro Castroviejo, needle tips oriented at 90 degrees pointing towards surgeon's dominant hand.
  - Suture boots must be clamped on all fine mosquito hemostats tagging monofilament Prolene (never bare metal).

## 2. Surgeon-Specific Preferences (Current Roster)
### Dr. Elena Rodriguez (Lap Chole)

- **Pledgets**: Pre-cut 4x4mm Teflon felt pledgets soaked in saline for aortic cannulation purse-strings.
