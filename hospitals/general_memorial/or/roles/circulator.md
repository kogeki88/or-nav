# Circulating RN — Case Governance, Positioning & Safety Guide
**Facility:** General Memorial Hospital | **Department:** Operating Room (OR)

## 1. Universal Protocol & Surgical Safety Timeout
- Complete pre-procedure sign-in before induction (identity, site mark, consent, allergies, anesthesia machine check).
- Formal Timeout executed immediately before skin incision: Surgeon, Scrub, Circulator, CRNA verbalize:
  - Patient name & procedure
  - Fire risk score (1-3)
  - Antibiotic redosing time
  - Anticipated blood loss & implants verified
- Post-procedure Sign-Out before cavity closure (sponge, needle, instrument count verified and documented).

## 2. Patient Positioning & Nerve Protection Matrix
- **Supine**: Arm boards padded, abduction angle < 90° to prevent brachial plexus stretch. Heels elevated off bed with gel pads.
- **Reverse Trendelenburg (Lap Chole)**: Foot board securely locked to table base with safety strap above knees.
- **Lateral / Prone**: Dependent axillary roll placed 3 finger breadths below axilla (never directly in axillary vault). Check dependent eye/ear for zero pressure.

## 3. Electrosurgical Unit (ESU) & Grounding Pad Rules
- Bovie return pad placed over well-vascularized muscular tissue (thigh or flank).
- Clean and dry skin before pad application; strictly avoid bony prominences, scar tissue, or proximity to orthopedic hardware/pacemakers.
- Smoke evacuator mandatory for all open and laparoscopic energy usage (OSHA/AORN compliance).

## 4. Specimen Chain of Custody
- Frozen Section: Dry sterile specimen container (NO formalin). Call Pathology extension **x4-1890** before runner handoff.
- Permanent Pathology: Fully submerged in 10% neutral buffered formalin (10:1 formalin to tissue ratio). Affix 2 patient ID stickers.
