# PACU & Pre-Operative Care Orientation Guide

## 1. Post-Anesthesia Recovery Standards & Aldrete Score
- **Aldrete Discharge Criteria**: Minimum score of **9/10** required for Phase I discharge to floor.
  1. **Activity**: Able to move 4 extremities spontaneously or on command (2).
  2. **Respiration**: Able to breathe deeply and cough freely (2).
  3. **Circulation**: BP within ± 20% of pre-op level (2).
  4. **Consciousness**: Fully awake (2).
  5. **O2 Saturation**: SpO2 > 94% on room air (2).

---

## 2. Neuromuscular Reversal & Sugammadex Protocol
- **Sugammadex (Bridion)**:
  - Moderate block (reappearance of T2): **2 mg/kg** actual body weight.
  - Deep block (1-2 post-tetanic counts): **4 mg/kg** actual body weight.
  - Immediate rescue reversal after 1.2 mg/kg Rocuronium: **16 mg/kg**.

---

## 3. Regional Anesthesia & LAST Protocol
- **Local Anesthetic Systemic Toxicity (LAST)**:
  - Seizure, arrhythmia, or cardiovascular collapse after nerve block.
  - **STAT Treatment**: 20% Intralipid kit located in Pre-Op Block Alcove Pyxis. Initial bolus **1.5 mL/kg** over 1 minute.
