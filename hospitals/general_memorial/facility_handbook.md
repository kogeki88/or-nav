# General Memorial Hospital — Clinical Employee Handbook & Emergency Policies

## 1. Emergency Crisis Codes & Immediate Extensions
- **Code Blue (Adult Cardiac/Respiratory Arrest)**: Call **x4-5555** or press bedside Code button. Team responds: ICU Attendee, Code RN, Respiratory Therapy, Anesthesia.
- **Code Red (Fire)**: R.A.C.E. (Rescue, Alarm, Contain, Extinguish). Alarm pull box by every stairwell and exit door.
- **Code Amber (Infant/Pediatric Abduction)**: Dial **x4-9911**. Immediate perimeter lockdown. Monitor all exits.
- **Code Silver (Active Threat/Shooter)**: A.D.D. (Avoid, Deny, Defend). Overhead broadcast to all floors.
- **Code Orange (Hazardous Material Spills)**: Evacuate room, close doors, dial Hazmat Response **x4-3344**.

## 3. Transfusion Service Governance & Chain of Custody
1. **Verification**: Two licensed healthcare clinicians (RN/MD/CRNA) must independently verify unit number, patient MRN, blood type (ABO/Rh compatibility), and expiration at patient bedside.
2. **Time to Infuse**: Transfusion MUST start within 30 minutes of leaving the blood bank cooler. If not initiated, return immediately to blood bank.
3. **Emergency Release**: Type O-Negative pRBC units available in Blood Bank Cooler Alpha without crossmatch (requires Attending MD signature within 4 hours).

## 4. Exposure Protocol & Occupational Health
- Immediate eye wash (15 minutes continuous rinse) or skin decontaminate with antimicrobial soap.
- Report within 1 hour to Employee Health (Room 104) or Emergency Department after hours (Ext: **x4-1100**).
- Rapid HIV PEP prophylactic initiation window < 2 hours post-exposure.
