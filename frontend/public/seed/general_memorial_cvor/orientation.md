# General Memorial CVOR — New Staff Orientation Guide

## 1. Station 12 Pneumatic Tube System

The CVOR complex uses **Station 12** for all pneumatic tube transactions. Know these rules cold — violations trigger incident reports.

### CAN Be Tubed
- Lab specimens (labeled, in biohazard bag, cap secured)
- Pharmacy medications (non-controlled, non-refrigerated)
- Small supply items under 5 lbs (e.g., suture packs, syringes)
- Paperwork and consent forms

### CANNOT Be Tubed — EVER
- **Blood products** — ALL blood products must be hand-carried by trained personnel. No exceptions.
- **Controlled substances** — Must be witnessed transport per DEA chain-of-custody.
- **Specimens in glass containers** — Breakage risk; hand-carry to lab.
- **Items exceeding carrier weight limit (5 lbs)** — Will jam the system.
- **Chemotherapy agents** — Spill risk; pharmacy courier only.

### Tube Station Location
- Station 12 is located in the **Core Supply corridor**, directly across from Cabinet D (medications). The keypad code is the same as the main OR entry code (changes quarterly — check with charge nurse).

## 2. Massive Transfusion Protocol (MTP)

Activation threshold: Anticipated need for ≥10 units pRBC in 24 hours, or active hemorrhage with hemodynamic instability unresponsive to crystalloid.

### Step-by-Step Activation
1. **Surgeon or anesthesiologist declares MTP** — verbal is sufficient initially.
2. **Call Blood Bank STAT**: Extension **x4-BLOOD (x4-25663)**. State: "MTP activation, OR 21, patient [name], blood type if known."
3. **First cooler arrives within 10 minutes** to OR suite. Runner meets at elevator bank B.
4. **Cooler contents (Round 1)**: 6 units pRBC, 4 units FFP, 1 unit platelets (6-pack).
5. **Component ratio target**: 1:1:1 (pRBC:FFP:Platelets) — per institutional protocol.
6. **Subsequent rounds**: Auto-delivered every 30 minutes until MTP is deactivated.
7. **Deactivation**: Surgeon or anesthesiologist calls blood bank: "MTP deactivated, final component tally requested."

### MTP Cooler Location
- Coolers are staged at **Elevator Bank B**, 2nd floor CVOR corridor. The MTP runner (assigned at start of shift) retrieves and delivers.

### Critical Lab Monitoring During MTP
- ABG, ionized calcium, fibrinogen, TEG/ROTEM — every 30 minutes minimum.
- Potassium monitoring critical with rapid transfusion (irradiated blood preferred if available).

## 3. Malignant Hyperthermia (MH) Cart

### Location
The MH Cart is stationed in the **main CVOR hallway between OR 20 and OR 21**, against the east wall. It is a bright **red cart** with "MALIGNANT HYPERTHERMIA" labels on all sides. **Do not move this cart or borrow supplies from it.**

### Dantrolene (Ryanodex) Quick Reference
- **Initial dose**: 2.5 mg/kg IV push, rapid bolus
- **Repeat**: 1 mg/kg every 5 minutes as needed; no maximum dose ceiling during acute crisis
- **Reconstitution**: Ryanodex — 250 mg vial, reconstitute with 5 mL sterile water (supplied in kit). Shake vigorously. Unlike legacy Dantrium, does NOT require 60 mL per vial.
- **Cart also contains**: Iced saline bags, core temperature probe, cooling blankets, bicarb, insulin/dextrose, calcium chloride.

### MH Hotline
**1-800-644-9737** (MHAUS 24/7 hotline) — Posted on cart and on the wall phone in each OR.

### Signs to Watch For
- Unexplained rising EtCO2 despite increased ventilation
- Jaw rigidity (masseter spasm) after succinylcholine
- Tachycardia, tachypnea, core temperature rising > 1°C every 5 minutes
- Mottled skin, generalized rigidity
- Dark urine (myoglobinuria) — late sign

## 4. Emergency Bypass / Crash-On Protocols

In the event of hemodynamic collapse requiring emergent cardiopulmonary bypass:

### Crash-On Checklist
1. **Perfusion STAT page**: Call switchboard "Perfusion STAT to OR [number]" — perfusionist responds within 3 minutes.
2. **Heparin**: Anesthesia administers **300 units/kg IV bolus** (full heparinization). Do NOT wait for ACT confirmation to cannulate if patient is coding.
3. **ACT Target**: ≥ 480 seconds for safe bypass. Draw ACT 3 minutes after heparin bolus.
4. **Cannulation**: Surgeon performs aortic + venous (right atrial two-stage or bicaval) cannulation.
5. **Pump on**: Perfusionist initiates bypass. Target flow 2.2-2.4 L/min/m² BSA.
6. **Verify**: MAP 60-80 mmHg on bypass, mixed venous O2 sat > 65%.

### Pre-Staged Emergency Circuit
- A **pre-primed bypass circuit** is maintained in Pump Room at all times during scheduled CVOR cases. Perfusion checks prime at start of day (0630) and after each case turnover.
