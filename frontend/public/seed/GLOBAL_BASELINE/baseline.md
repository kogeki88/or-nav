# GLOBAL BASELINE: AORN / CNOR / CST Perioperative Reference Guide

> **CLINICAL GOVERNANCE NOTICE**: This document contains standard educational perioperative nursing and surgical technology baselines (AORN Guidelines for Perioperative Practice, CNOR, and CST standards). **Always defer to local Hospital Policy and attending surgeon directive when facility protocols differ.**

## 1. Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG) Standard Checkpoints
- **Patient Positioning**: Supine with shoulder roll (if requested for sternal exposure); arms tucked neutrally at sides with ulnar nerve foam protection (palms facing thighs, thumbs up); heel offloading boots applied prior to drape.
- **Surgical Prep Boundaries**: Chin to bilateral knees (or bilateral ankles if greater saphenous vein endoscopic/open harvest is planned), and laterally to the mid-axillary lines bilaterally.
- **Bladder Decompression & Temperature**: Indwelling Foley catheter with temperature-sensing probe inserted prior to prep; verify urine output monitor visibility for anesthesia and perfusion.
- **Standard Instrumentation & Conduit Prep**: Sternal saw (oscillating for redo sternotomy), internal mammary artery (IMA) retractor, Castroviejo needle holders, Potts scissors (45° and 60°), coronary dilators (1.0mm–2.5mm), and heparinized papaverine/blood solution for IMA conduit storage.

## 2. Aortic & Mitral Valve Replacement (AVR / MVR) Standard Checkpoints
- **Positioning & Defibrillation Readiness**: Supine position; external R2 defibrillation pads placed anterolaterally on all redo sternotomy and minimally invasive valve cases prior to skin prep. Internal defibrillator paddles (spoons) verified at **20 Joules** before aortic cross-clamp.
- **Prep Boundaries**: Chin to bilateral groins (ensuring femoral artery/vein exposure for potential peripheral cannulation).
- **Valve Implantation Safeguards**:
  - Verify prosthetic valve serial number, size (mm), and expiration date with circulating RN and attending surgeon before opening inner sterile seal.
  - Bioprosthetic (glutaraldehyde-preserved) valves require sequential sterile saline rinses per manufacturer IFU (typically 3 separate basins × 60 seconds) unless labeled rinse-free (e.g., Inspiris Resilia).
  - Double-armed braided polyester sutures (`2-0 Ti-Cron` with PTFE pledgets) organized sequentially on valve suture ring; strict needle and pledget count reconciliation before aortotomy/atriotomy closure.

## 3. Carotid Endarterectomy (CEA) & Cranial Nerve Checkpoints
- **Patient Positioning**: Supine with head rested on foam donut or gel headrest, neck slightly extended and rotated **15°–30° contralaterally** away from the operative side; avoid excessive cervical hyperextension.
- **Surgical Prep Boundaries**: Ear lobe and angle of mandible superiorly, across midline to contralateral sternocleidomastoid, and inferiorly to the suprasternal notch / upper chest.
- **Intraoperative Neuro & Shunt Checkpoints**:
  - Maintain normotension or slight permissive hypertension during carotid cross-clamping to preserve cerebral collateral perfusion via the Circle of Willis.
  - Have Javid or Pruitt-Inahara carotid shunt primed and immediately available on the sterile field prior to arteriotomy.
  - **Post-Debrief Cranial Nerve Assessment (CEA)**:
    - **CN VII (Facial Nerve — Marginal Mandibular Branch)**: Assess symmetry of smile / lower lip depression.
    - **CN X (Vagus / Recurrent Laryngeal Nerve)**: Assess voice quality for hoarseness and intact swallow reflex.
    - **CN XII (Hypoglossal Nerve)**: Ask patient to protrude tongue; deviation **toward** the operative side indicates ipsilateral hypoglossal nerve traction or injury.

## 4. Universal AORN Perioperative Safety & Count Checkpoints
- **Electrosurgery (ESU) Safety**: Place dispersive return electrode (grounding pad) over a clean, dry, well-vascularized muscular mass close to the operative site, avoiding bony prominences, scar tissue, and orthopedic implants. Keep alcohol-based skin prep (ChloraPrep) drying time strictly **≥ 3 minutes** (or until completely dry) to prevent surgical fire.
- **Surgical Count Phases**: Perform audible, visual concurrent counts (Sponges, Sharps, Miscellaneous Small Items, and Instruments) at 4 mandatory intervals:
  1. Prior to case start (Baseline).
  2. Before closure of a cavity within a cavity (e.g., pericardium or pleura).
  3. Before wound closure begins (sternal wire approximation).
  4. At skin closure or end of procedure.
